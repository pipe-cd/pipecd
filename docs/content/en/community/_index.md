## Community meetings

The PipeCD Development and Community Meeting runs every two weeks on Wednesday at 16:30 JST (07:30 UTC). We share project news and demos, answer questions, and triage issues. Everyone is welcome.

- **Join the meeting**: [Zoom](https://zoom-lfx.platform.linuxfoundation.org/meeting/96831504919?password=2f60b8ec-5896-40c8-aa1d-d551ab339d00)
- **Meeting notes and agenda**: [Google Doc](https://bit.ly/pipecd-mtg-notes)
- **Add to my calendar**: <a href="webcal://webcal.prod.itx.linuxfoundation.org/lfx/a092M00001MtBxUQAV">Subscribe (iCal)</a>. For Google Calendar, add `https://webcal.prod.itx.linuxfoundation.org/lfx/a092M00001MtBxUQAV` with "From URL".
